Your photos, on e-paper.

FolioFrame is a connected photo frame built around a 7.09″ Spectra 6 color e-ink display. It sips power, looks like a printed photograph, and is managed entirely from the web.

Open the web app View source
7.09″ Spectra 6 e-paper
six inks · 1200 × 1600
no backlight, no glare

Designed around your library

No subscriptions, no walled garden. Point FolioFrame at the photos you already have.

Web photo management

Browse, organize, and push photos to the frame from any browser. Your library combines Google Photos and direct uploads in one place.

Simple pairing

Claim a frame with a pairing code in under a minute. Frames stay linked to your account across firmware updates.

Wireless firmware updates

New features and display improvements arrive over the air, verified before install with a battery safety gate.

Open-source firmware

The device firmware is open source on GitHub — auditable, hackable, and built in the open.

E-paper, not LCD

Spectra 6 renders photos in six inks with a paper-like finish. It holds an image with zero power and causes no eye strain.

Self-hostable service

The companion web service is Dockerized and self-hostable, so your photos stay under your control.

Under the glass

Honest hardware, documented in the open.
